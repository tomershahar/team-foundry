# Outcomes — Q2 2026

## Validated outcomes

### O1 — AP leads process month-end in under 2 days

Baseline: 4.1 days average (cohort data, Q1 2026). Target: ≤2 days for customers with <200 invoices/month.
Signal: time-to-close in approval routing data, tracked per cohort.
Why it matters: month-end duration is the #1 complaint in NPS verbatims this quarter.

### O2 — New customers reach first auto-approved invoice within 5 days

Baseline: 6.2 days (onboarding funnel, Q1 2026). Target: ≤5 days.
Signal: time-to-first-value in onboarding funnel (tracked per cohort).
Why it matters: customers who hit first value fast have 3× better 90-day retention (cohort analysis, David, Mar 2026).

### O3 — Finance managers trust the exception queue enough to stop spot-checking

Baseline: 6/10 customers manually verify auto-approvals (customer interviews, Sarah, Apr 2026).
Target: 8/10 say they no longer spot-check in 30-day post-adoption interview.
Signal: post-adoption interview score for customers on the new exception UI.
Why it matters: if they're spot-checking, we haven't solved the trust problem — we've just moved the work.

## Hypothesized outcomes

### O4 — One enterprise customer reaches 80% auto-processing rate (stretch)

Why we believe it: three enterprise prospects have asked about auto-processing rates explicitly.
What would validate it: one customer on multi-approver workflow hits 80% in their first full quarter.
What we need first: multi-approver workflow (not yet built). Only pursue if O1–O3 on track by mid-quarter.

### Not this quarter

- Reducing support ticket volume (consequence, not target — we're watching it)
- International invoice formats (deferred; only 3 customers have asked)
- Mobile app (not enough demand signal to justify the context-switch cost)
